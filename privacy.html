Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ow Fyah ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile games and apps we publish on Google Play (the "Apps").

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email or contacts through the Apps. Game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising

Some Apps show ads using AppLovin MAX and its partner ad networks. To show and measure ads, these providers may automatically collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (model, operating system, language, approximate location based on IP address)
  • Information about ad interactions (impressions, clicks)

This information is processed according to the providers' own privacy policies, including AppLovin's Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Analytics and crash reports

Google Play may provide us with anonymous statistics and crash reports to help us improve the Apps.

Children

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is only shared with the advertising and service providers described above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
